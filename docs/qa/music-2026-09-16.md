# Owned music beds

Private resumable library accepts MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC and OGG music with rights attribution. Music cannot be selected as video B-roll; project ownership and offset bounds are checked on save and render. Existing video assets remain compatible.

The manual editor supports selection/removal, source offset, -40 to -6 dB gain, fades, and optional ducking from the original mixed audio. A selected excerpt repeats to the approved output duration. Original video is preserved during mixing; a limiter prevents excessive summed levels. Silent source videos acquire the music track. Director Timeline exposes the bed. Creative replanning preserves saved music and cannot invent an unseen music asset.

This is a manual music foundation, not AI emotional matching, music generation, licensing search, source separation, or beat-aligned cutting. Ducking responds to original mixed audio, not isolated voice.
