Existing external B-roll may be retained with exactly the same insert parameters and parent source bounds on unlocked shots. New asset selection or relocation remains prohibited without visual matching. Locked-shot rules remain unchanged. Acceptance now revalidates asset ownership/availability before writing the edit. An unlocked scene can still be removed or replaced in the proposed plan; this is not an unconditional B-roll lock.

Validation covers corrected captions surviving generation and acceptance, empty captions not being restored, retained B-roll and rejected relocation, plus existing creative-plan and quality-revision regressions. Provider output is mocked; no paid or customer rerenders.
