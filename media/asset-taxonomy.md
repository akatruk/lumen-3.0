# Visual asset taxonomy

Source: `Hayek-Law-English.mp4`, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 58.1s. Twelve frames were sampled, one every five seconds. The file is a visual-direction reference only. Its name, logo, and wording are not part of this library.

## What the picture is made of

The film is not one generated scene. It is a dark field, a talking head, and a small set of separable objects that repeat:

- A full-frame host, then the same host cut into a circle in the corner.
- One large object in the middle: a target, a stack of cards, a wireframe globe, or a photograph.
- A row of three small line icons, or a vertical list of five steps with a tiny icon on each row.
- Two tiles side by side (briefcase, chart) above a lifestyle photograph.
- Hairline arcs in the background. They are decoration, not content.
- Type is always drawn on top. The pictures themselves are objects.

That split is the system. Remotion should own the type, the cards, the list, and the motion. The asset library should own the objects.

## Recurring categories

### Line icons

Thin stroke, one weight, gold or cool blue, sitting in a rounded tile. Seen concepts: aircraft, house, globe, target, two people, wallet, chat, document, check. They are symbols, not illustrations. They must stay small and identical in weight so a row of three still reads.

### Center illustrations

One object, lots of empty field around it. The target-with-arrow and the wireframe globe are the same idea at a larger size: a single metaphor you can scale and fade. They are not posters.

### Lifestyle stills

Real photographs, cropped wide, with the subject off to one side or in the lower half so type can sit above. Two uses showed up: a consultation across a table, and a family outside a house. They are labeled in the reference as footage, which means they are plates, not finished frames.

### Document objects

A fanned stack of pale cards. The only mark that matters is a check. Any words on those cards in the reference are a mistake for us: Remotion will label them. Our version is blank rules and a check.

### Global diagrams

A sphere of latitude lines, a few dots, one dashed route. No map labels. Remotion can draw extra routes later; the asset is the sphere and the first path.

### UI chrome that is not an asset

Rounded rectangles, pills, progress ticks, and numbered rows are layout. They belong in the motion components, not in generated images. Baking a card around an icon makes it impossible to retint or reflow.

### Speaker

The host is source video, masked by the scene (`circle`, `portrait`, `fullscreen`). No generated face stands in for the host.

## Style rules taken from the reference

- One dark field. Objects sit on it; they do not bring their own scenery, except lifestyle photographs.
- Two accents only: a warm gold line and a cool blue line. A single green check is the only third color.
- Geometry is simple. Circles, houses, planes, wallets. No texture, no gloss, no 3D emoji.
- Each beat changes the object, not the chrome. Target, then photograph plus list, then paper stack, then family photograph, then globe, then the host again.

## What we will not copy

The wordmark, the pill logo, the phrase lockups, and any readable type on documents.
