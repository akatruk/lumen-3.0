# Three directions

Stills are Remotion frames at 1080×1920 with the real editorial geometry, a source frame in the PIP, and the real caption line. Paths:

`output/motion-art-direction/concept-a/`
`output/motion-art-direction/concept-b/`
`output/motion-art-direction/concept-c/`

Each folder has `timeline-initial.png`, `timeline-active.png`, `timeline-complete.png`, `range-initial.png`, `range-active.png`, `range-complete.png`.

These notes are engineering judgments from those frames, not a preference score.

## A — editorial type

Timeline complete: 使用场景 and 家庭结构 small, 代价 large, one left edge, no stem. The stack still sits in the upper left. The right two-thirds of the graphic is empty.

Range complete: 400 and 1200 are large and far apart. The dash between them is small, so the relationship is weaker than the numbers. 每天 and 人民币 are present. No invented unit.

## B — spatial object

Timeline complete: three rows share the graphic height. Hairlines run the width of the band. 代价 carries a gold mark. The words are the source three. No 步骤.

Timeline initial: one word in the first row. Empty rules under it read as a form. The implementation therefore draws a rule only between rows that are already spoken.

Range complete: 400 and 1200 sit on the ends of one line that spans the graphic. 每天 sits in the gap. 人民币 sits under the line. The active still shows the line growing from 400 toward an empty end, so the high value is not on screen before the beat reveals it.

## C — cinematic stage

Timeline complete: a small index of all three words, and 代价 alone at display size. That is the oversized single word again. The index is too small to carry the structure.

Range complete: 400 is upper left, 1200 is lower right, and the line does not meet either figure. They read as two separate numbers.

## Why B

A restates the left cluster. C breaks the range into two places. B is the only study where both components are one object: a stack the height of the graphic, and a scale the width of the graphic. Caption and PIP in the stills stay in the existing boxes.
